# Qwen3-14B native AV: offline failure and capture audit

**Disposition:** native-layout AV remains experimental and `Candidate_NOT_Valid`; canonical packed-V AV remains authoritative. This audit adds no execution path, threshold, admission rule, guard, or production behavior. It used only committed captures, logs, manifests, and CPU reference replay; no new model inference or GPU workload was launched.

## What was audited

The audit is tied to the Qwen3-14B payload SHA-256 `f409ec946faf59cb338647c47efc2f28e9bdec7e8bd33acacd0cd6a36f2eaa31`, source GGUF SHA-256 `915913e22399475dbe6c968ac014d9f1fbe08975e489279aede9d5c7b2c98eb6`, GGML commit `2d191b5dee1a591c41ee8a653ce42bfcd9c8716d`, and placement `multi:0x26,1x14;emb=0;norm=1;head=1`. The current candidate guard and final-output contract were not changed.

The reproducible index is generated by `integrations/ggml/qualification/qwen3_native_av_evidence_audit.py`. Its outputs are under [`raw/offline-capture-audit-20261010/`](qwen3-numerical-propagation/raw/offline-capture-audit-20261010/):

- `fixture-inventory.csv` records comparison scope, capacity, phase/topology, exact-input and topology equivalence, repeat count, evidence path, and one of `PASS`, `FAIL`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `NOT_TESTED`, or `INVALID_EVIDENCE`.
- `capture-integrity.csv` records metadata and binary SHA-256, exact expected byte count, layout, and duplicate-capture comparison.
- `audit-summary.json` records the inventory counts. There are 70 comparison/control records: 35 PASS, 10 FAIL, 5 NOT_APPLICABLE, 19 NOT_TESTED, and one INVALID_EVIDENCE. These are not 70 independent model qualifications; the inventory includes repeated rows, topology controls, and byte-integrity controls.
- `reference-replay.log` is the offline FP64 replay output; `capture-tamper-negative-control.txt` records an expected rejection of a deliberately corrupted temporary capture.

### Original matrix and comparison validity

The original versioned matrix planned 20 fixtures and stopped at the first new failure. Only its baseline was run: the 8-token + 25-generated-token incremental fixture passed twice, while a common-token 32-row-prefill + position-32 replay failed twice. The audit reconstructed the exact 33-token input to the common replay from the manifest prompt IDs plus the free-running generated IDs and confirmed it equals the replay’s recorded input IDs. The A/B paths within each mode have the same inputs and topology; the incremental and batched modes differ in segmentation and are not an identical-topology comparison. The other 19 original rows remain **NOT_TESTED**; separate follow-ups do not backfill them.

The isolated phase comparison uses one identical 33-token history, capacity 512, and position-32 endpoint in both arms. Native prefill followed by canonical decode fails the unchanged output gate (logits relative RMS `0.0225540`); canonical prefill followed by native decode passes (`0.0146598`). Both repeated twice. This supports a prefill-sensitive compatibility issue on that history, not a general proof that all native decode paths pass.

Other pertinent outcomes, all retained separately in the inventory:

- Same-history topology control, repeated twice: canonical incremental vs canonical batched logits relative RMS `0.0184071` (pass); native incremental vs native batched `0.0268631` (fail). Candidate-vs-canonical incremental is `0.0170682` (pass), while candidate-vs-canonical batched is `0.0236490` (fail). The canonical control itself has a measurable topology effect.
- The exact 9-token short prefix is bitwise identical between the short and long incremental sessions for both canonical and native outputs. The short candidate-vs-canonical comparison at position 8 still fails (`0.0301166` logits relative RMS); the longer trajectory’s final position-32 endpoint passes. This is non-monotonic propagation, not a different token history or a session/capture mismatch.
- The 20 single-layer intervention records contain 19 passes and one fail: layer 0 at decode position 8 (`0.0201839` logits relative RMS, cosine `0.999796`).
- Two separate capacity-512 post-stop fixtures pass twice: natural 32-row prefill + one decode and a synthetic repeated-token history. Neither resolves the original same-history failures.
- A historical capacity-64 runner row is recorded as `INVALID_EVIDENCE` **for its status classification only**: that runner collapsed policy `NOT_APPLICABLE` into `NUMERIC_GATE_FAIL`. The corrected control reports `NOT_APPLICABLE`, as required by policy v2’s minimum capacity of 512. The measured values remain diagnostic, not a gate pass or failure.

Historical capacity endpoints are also indexed: capacities 64 and 256 had recorded passes but are outside the current policy-v2 scope; 512 is in scope and had a recorded pass; capacities 1024 and 1032 had pre-guard failures but are not admitted by the current candidate guard. The latter failures must not be mistaken for current eligible Native AV fixtures.

## Offline capture and high-precision replay

The layer-0 capacity-512 captures have exact expected payload sizes of 3,571,840 bytes (32-row prefill) and 243,492 bytes (one-row decode). Their binary SHA-256 values are:

- Prefill: `b53f5999707d8dd8532fa6af41a09f8aa01e605cdddb75058cb956078dc96263`
- Decode: `13dbe659c5f437a31439d6cd33f97c35f7d4961d0d59b45df15ded69bc9e0ca2`

The primary and repeat sweeps, plus the corresponding native-prefill locality capture, are byte-identical at capacity 512. All audited files pass geometry, metadata, and exact byte-length checks. The v1 capture metadata does not embed model or token identity; association to the model/prompt therefore comes from the preserved runner logs and manifests rather than from the binary alone.

The enhanced `vbuf_qwen3_reference_capture_qualification` replays the capture with `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES` empty. It independently reproduces the saved FP64 QK oracle, both saved softmax reference arrays, and the AV oracle bitwise. The tamper control flips one bit in a temporary `softmax_from_scores_F32` value and the replay rejects it at element 0, demonstrating that the new section-integrity check is active.

Layer-0 operation diagnostics (all replay-only; contracts remain `NEEDS_CALIBRATION` / `NOT_TESTED`):

| Captured phase | QK CUDA vs FP64 QK | CUDA P vs FP64 softmax of captured CUDA QK | CUDA P vs FP64 softmax of FP64 QK | Native AV vs FP64 AV | Canonical AV vs FP64 AV |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Prefill, 32 rows | `5.2141e-4` on causal-active scores (`5.1697e-4` if masked-future score slots are included) | `1.1153e-7` | `1.9014e-3` | `4.2341e-8` | `2.6374e-4` |
| Decode, context 33 | `1.3394e-4` | `1.2775e-7` | `3.0318e-4` | `5.0257e-8` | `2.4018e-4` |

The causal-active QK metric is reported separately because future-position scores in a prefill row are masked and do not participate in attention. The softmax result shows that CUDA softmax closely normalizes the scores it receives; the larger P-vs-oracle-QK difference includes upstream QK rounding. Neither QK nor softmax difference explains a candidate-vs-canonical discrepancy at the same capacity: those operations and their inputs are common to both AV arms in the layer-0 locality capture.

### Earliest meaningful divergence

The locality capture records layer-0 V, P, Q, K, active scores, and positions as bitwise identical between the native candidate graph and canonical replay; the actual candidate native output also matches the same-input native side branch. Yet native-vs-canonical AV differs by `2.63736e-4` relative RMS. Native AV is much closer to the independent FP64 AV result (`4.2341e-8`) than canonical packed-V AV (`2.6374e-4`) on this sampled input.

This makes the first meaningful candidate/canonical difference **AV accumulation arithmetic**, not QK, softmax, GQA indexing, cache layout, or capture serialization. The native CUDA kernel uses `kv_h = floor(h/5)`, reads the captured native V layout, and accumulates visible positions in ascending order with the kernel's F32 `sum += weight * v` operation. The independent oracle uses the same captured F16 V and F32 probabilities with ascending-position FP64 accumulation. This bounded check supports mathematical correctness of the sampled native AV result; it does not prove universal correctness or numerical compatibility with the canonical reduction.

After AV, layer-0 block hidden state differs by `0.00390628` relative RMS. At layer 1, AV inputs are no longer bitwise equal, consistent with propagation from layer 0; later AV-local metrics are not same-input operation comparisons. The final-output failures are therefore compatible with a small, mathematically valid reduction-order perturbation amplified by downstream computation and phase/topology interaction. They do not establish a native AV indexing or reference-harness defect.

A separate, previously measured capacity effect must be kept distinct: at capacities 1024/1032, layer-0 decode QK scores differ across capacities despite identical active Q/K; pinned-source dispatch changes from MMVF through 512 to MMF at 1024 and cuBLAS at 1032. Softmax still accurately normalizes the received scores. Those historical out-of-guard observations are upstream of AV and do not explain the eligible capacity-512 same-history prefill failure.
